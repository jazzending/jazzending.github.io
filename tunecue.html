WHAT IT DOES

From a track
to a useful starting point.

Upload a reference track and TuneCue helps make its musical qualities easier to describe: mood, tempo, sound palette, and arrangement direction. Those cues are then shaped into an English prompt for AI music generators.

Made for music makers with a clear taste, even without music theory or production jargon.

    Start with a song that has the feeling or sound direction you want to explore.

  2. 02

    Read the musical cues

    TuneCue describes practical cues such as tempo, mood, groove, texture, and the way the arrangement builds.

  3. 03

    Copy an AI music prompt

    Switch between a simple or detailed version, then use the prompt as a more informed starting point for a new track.
